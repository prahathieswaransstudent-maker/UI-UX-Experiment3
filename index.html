Nielsen’s 10 heuristics

Competitive UX review

Evaluation of two fictional shopping experiences based on common patterns observed in established e-commerce apps.

A

ShopSphere

Original e-commerce experience

  • Strength: Clear category hierarchy and familiar cart placement.
  • Issue: Small search affordance and no visible result count.
  • Severity: Medium — 3/4.
  • Fix: Add a prominent labelled search and persistent filtered context.
B

MarketCart

Busy marketplace experience

  • Strength: Strong promotions and dense product discovery.
  • Issue: Weak visual hierarchy and inconsistent add-to-cart placement.
  • Severity: High — 4/4.
  • Fix: Simplify hierarchy and place the primary action consistently.
